### Description
A postgraduate course on the theory of data science and its application to civil and transportation
engineering problems. It covers supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, and demonstrates
these methods on spatio-temporal traffic state prediction, human mobility pattern discovery, traffic
congestion identification and characterisation, traffic incident analysis, and the operation and
control of smart mobility systems. Students learn to formulate real-world engineering applications as
data science problems and to compare competing approaches against common performance criteria.
