### Description
A postgraduate course on the NEC suite of construction contracts, centred on the NEC Engineering and
Construction Contract (ECC) Hong Kong Edition. Topics include Z clauses and practice notes, tender
strategy and documentation, the obligations of mutual trust and co-operation, communication and early
warning, programme and defects, payment and compensation events, title, liabilities and insurance,
termination, key performance indicators, early contractor involvement, climate change, and avoiding
and resolving disputes.
